Meet the founder

Kathim Mualla

Before ProDent existed, Kathim began chairside as a dental assistant in a newly purchased dental practice. The view from chairside taught him the clinical rhythm. The move to the front desk taught him the commercial reality.

He taught himself Google Ads and digital marketing, moved into practice management, and within his first year was running the business side of the operation. After that first acquisition, he partnered with his brother to open three more practices in only a few years. He later went on to open tens of dental practice startups successfully.

That experience matters because practices are not case studies. They are busy, human systems. Every recommendation ProDent makes is filtered through one question: Will this actually hold up on a Tuesday afternoon with a full schedule and an understaffed front desk?
