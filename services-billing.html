Why it matters

What good billing looks like

Credentialing done right

We fill out the applications, chase the payers, and keep at it until you're in-network with the companies you want. Full credentialing typically runs about $2,100.

Claims that get paid

We submit clean claims, chase the ones that stall, and work the denials instead of writing them off. Nothing sits in a queue for weeks.

Smoother patient billing

Statements patients can read, reminders that don't feel like threats, and collections help that doesn't cost you the relationship.

Verification before the visit

We check eligibility and benefits before the appointment, so nobody finds out at checkout that something isn't covered.
