Your project timeline

Mapped out in writing before the build starts

Early in the engagement you get a written visual project timeline — every phase, milestone, and build-out payment date mapped out in detail. Here's the shape of a typical 4–6 month launch.

Phase 1

Plan & secure

Lease negotiated and signed, financing lined up for your startup loan, entity formed, licensing and permits underway — and your written project timeline delivered, with every phase, milestone, and payment date on it.

Phase 2

Build & equip

Construction, office design, signage, equipment deals, utilities, and IT — contractors and vendors held to one schedule, with us negotiating every contract on your behalf.

Phase 3

Staff & systems

Practice software installed and configured, phones and digital forms ready, vendor portals and lab accounts open. Your team is hired and trained on your systems, and credentialing applications are working through your chosen networks.

Phase 4

Launch & fill

Brand and website live, Google Business Profile and social accounts up, Google Ads running, and the grand opening event planned and promoted — so opening week isn't an empty schedule.

After opening

90 days of post-launch advisory

We don't hand over the keys and disappear. For 90 days after launch we stay on as advisors — and your first two months of Google Ads management and insurance billing are already included, so the practice starts on working systems.
