Coached through every area

The same six workstreams as the turnkey build

On the turnkey launch we execute all six for you. On the DIY track you execute — and get coached through the decisions in each one.

Build-out

Lease, office design & construction planning, signage, equipment choices, utilities & IT. You run the project; we tell you what good looks like at each step.

Business & legal

Entity formation, office licensing & permits, business insurance, employment contracts, and OSHA & HIPAA compliance. You file; we make sure nothing gets missed.

Financial

Startup-loan financing, fee schedules & PPO participation strategy, payroll & benefits, merchant services, and patient financing — decisions you'll live with for years.

Systems

Practice management software, digital patient forms, phones & VoIP, vendor portals, and lab accounts. You set them up; we steer you to the right picks.

Team

Hiring your front desk, assistants, and associates with our templates, plus training plans and insurance credentialing direction — so the team is ready for day one.

Marketing & launch

Brand, website, Google Business Profile, social accounts, and the opening push — we set the direction so opening week isn't an empty schedule.
